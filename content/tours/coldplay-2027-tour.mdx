## Quick answer

**No official 2027 dates exist yet.** Coldplay's own tour page states plainly: "There are no dates currently scheduled." That's straight from coldplay.com, not a rumor site.

That said, this isn't a cold trail. Chris Martin told fans directly during the band's September 2025 Wembley shows that the Music Of The Spheres World Tour is far from over and that 2027 shows would be announced. That's the strongest signal available — an on-record statement from the band's frontman — but it is still not a confirmed schedule.

## What Chris Martin actually said

At Wembley Stadium in September 2025, Martin told the crowd the tour still had roughly 138 shows left to go, framing it as a long way from finished.

> We have a break coming up, and we'll announce the shows for 2027 soon.
> — Chris Martin

That's a real, on-record commitment to a 2027 return — just without dates, cities, or a timeline for the announcement itself.

## Expected regions (based on touring history, not confirmed plans)

The Music Of The Spheres World Tour, running since March 2022, has already crossed North America, the UK, Europe, Australia, and parts of Asia multiple times. If the pattern holds for a 2027 continuation, markets that haven't been revisited as recently — parts of Latin America, the Middle East, and additional Asian cities — would be reasonable guesses. None of this is confirmed; it's pattern-based speculation only.

## Sustainability, for context

Coldplay has built this tour cycle around emissions reduction — kinetic dance floors, rechargeable show batteries, and published emissions reports are part of the Music Of The Spheres era's identity. That's factual and ongoing, but it's not a signal about 2027 dates specifically — just context for what a continuation would likely carry forward.
