## Frequently asked questions

**Is Garth Brooks touring in 2027?**
Not confirmed. His current tour, Blame It All On My Roots, is a 2026 arena tour, and no 2027 dates have been announced.

**What is the Blame It All On My Roots Tour?**
A 2026 return to arena touring, reviving Brooks' famous 1990s "Drum Pod" in-the-round stage setup. It's tied to a new live recording project called Killer Live, and follows a sold-out show at London's Hyde Park.

**Which cities are confirmed for 2026?**
The tour opened with two shows in Indianapolis (Gainbridge Fieldhouse, August 21–22), followed by Denver, then Chicago's Allstate Arena (September 11–12) — with more cities being added as the tour continues.

**How much do tickets cost?**
A flat $154 (including fees) for every seat — Brooks' team has been explicit that this is a deliberate single-price structure, not tiered pricing.

**Is there a presale?**
No — all tickets go on sale simultaneously via Ticketmaster, with no presale or advance box office sales.

**Is toursetlist.com's Garth Brooks 2027 page accurate?**
No. It lists four Indianapolis dates (Aug 20–23) that don't match the actual official announcement of two shows (Aug 21–22) — a factual error that also shows up in this site's coverage of other artists.
