## Quick answer

**Not confirmed** — Oasis's official site currently lists no dates beyond their completed Live '25 reunion tour, which wrapped in São Paulo on November 23, 2025. There is no announced 2027 schedule, no cities, and no ticket on-sale.

That said, this one has more real smoke than most "unconfirmed tour" pages. Liam Gallagher has directly, publicly ruled out a 2026 tour while leaving 2027 open — and later posted a cryptic tease widely read as a nod to 2027 shows. Neither is an official announcement, but both are real, on-record statements worth knowing about.

## What Liam has actually said

<Callout type="rumor" title="⚠️ Real statements, not confirmation">
Asked directly on social media whether there'd be a 2026 tour, Gallagher said no, then called 2027 a "maybe." Weeks later, responding to a fan asking about new music, he wrote a cryptic line read by outlets as a hint toward 2027 shows.
</Callout>

> Maybe.
> — Liam Gallagher, on the possibility of a 2027 tour

Noel Gallagher, for his part, has stayed quiet on specifics — consistent with his general pattern of not discussing Oasis plans in interviews. Neither brother, nor any official Oasis channel, has confirmed dates, cities, or even that a tour is definitely happening.

## Fake sites are already inventing details — here's what to ignore

<Callout type="warning" title="🚫 Not real">
Several sites (including ones ranking for "Oasis 2027 tour") are currently claiming a "12-night residency at Manchester's Etihad Stadium" and that Liam has "officially confirmed" a 2027 tour. Neither claim is corroborated by any real source — not Oasis's official site, not Ticketmaster, not any credible outlet. The Etihad detail in particular appears to be invented outright; there is no report of it anywhere in legitimate music press.
</Callout>

## Where things likely stand

Given Liam's public no on 2026 and the brothers' history of publicly feuding even during a successful reunion, a 2027 tour is plausible but genuinely uncertain — this isn't a band with a predictable release cadence. If it happens, expect it to follow the reunion tour's stadium-scale pattern, but there's no basis yet for guessing specific cities.
